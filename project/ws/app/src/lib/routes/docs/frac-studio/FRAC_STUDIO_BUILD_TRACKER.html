How a review runs, first to last

The Reviewer upload and the Creator's expert check enter separately, become the same review model, and run through one engine. Everything above the AI box is counted in code and gives the same answer every time.

Every computed check

Severity: B Blocker, M Major, m Minor. Checks marked "FRAC sheet" run only when the workbook has one.

CheckRuleSeverity
Bloom's verbs A level label starts with a non-measurable verb (Understands, Knows, Learns, Is aware of, Appreciates, Is familiar with, Realises, Comprehends). Counted per level; adds "Levels 1 and 2 barely differ" when both are hit. M if the worst level hits ≥25% of competencies, else m
Labels are clean but an indicator starts with one of those verbs. m
FRAC structure Activities start with a syllabus verb (Describes, Explains, Lists, Defines, States, Discusses…). FRAC sheet. M at ≥30% of activities, else m
A role has fewer than 2 activities. FRAC sheet. m
No competency is typed Domain, Functional or Behavioural. M
No Behavioural or no Functional competencies (KCM has 13 B + 21 F). M
Level ladder A competency has fewer than 5 level labels. M above 10% of competencies, else m
The same L5 label appears on 3 or more competencies (one finding per label). M
One competency repeats a label across two of its levels. m
Coverage & sources Activities with no competency mapping; the finding quotes the first 3. FRAC sheet. M at ≥5% of activities, else m
Competencies no activity or role maps to. FRAC sheet. m
Mappings point to codes missing from the competency sheet. FRAC sheet. M
Some, but not all, activities have every posting column blank. FRAC sheet. m
Guidelines & context Repealed laws: Lunacy Act or "lunatic", Mental Health Act 1987, NDPS … 1965. Searched across activities, competencies and every level. One finding per pattern, up to 12 codes listed. B
TT or tetanus toxoid (now Td, 2018). M
Old programme names: RNTCP, NPCDCS, HWC / Health & Wellness Centre, NVBDCP, MCTS, MTP Act 1971 unless "2021" appears nearby. m
AI review pending Real job areas vs curriculum subjects, observable activities, granularity, ladder progression, out-of-scope acts, wrong laws, drugs, doses or protocols, state fit, Karmayogi alignment. At most 10 findings; Blocker means unsafe, illegal or factually wrong. B M m
